Purchases

If premium access is missing after reinstalling or changing devices, open Sidequest settings and use Restore Purchases.

Photos and history

Quest history, notes, badges, and chronicle entries are stored on your device. Chronicle photos use your iOS Photos library.

Quest safety

Use judgement, follow local laws, and skip any quest that feels unsafe, illegal, or inappropriate for your situation.

Quest packs

Sidequest may download the latest quest pool from the Sidequest quest repository. If the network is unavailable, the app can use bundled or cached quests.

Widget and Live Activity

If an active quest is not visible in the widget or Live Activity, open the app once and start or restore the current quest.

FAQ

Can I block quests I do not want?

Yes. In settings, you can disable categories or add blocked keywords.

Which languages are supported?

Sidequest is localized in English, Czech, and Slovak.

Does Sidequest have a widget?

Yes. The widget and Live Activity can show your current active quest at a glance.

What does Premium unlock?

Premium unlocks Bat Shit Crazy difficulty, exclusive BSC quest drops, and future quest packs.
